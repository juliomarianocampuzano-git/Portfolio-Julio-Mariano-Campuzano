PromptPro

Turn a loose idea into a professional instruction for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other AI. It builds text and image prompts, audits the ones you already use and keeps your history.

HTMLJavaScriptAI
Try demoOpen full screen

What it solves

  • Turns an idea into a prompt with role, context, task, rules and output format.
  • Builds image prompts and audits the ones you already use, with a score and an improved version.
  • Works without a key in demo mode; with your own AI it writes, translates and tests the result.

To protect the work, this page shows what the project solves, not how it is built inside.
