Data & accounting

Contador LIGHT

An accounting workstation that installs on a notebook and works without Internet: it reads the client's bank statements, prepares the entries and the Excel workbook for each fiscal year, and adds an AI assistant. Everything stays a draft for the professional to review.

PythonAIDataExcel
See screenshots

What it solves

  • Reads bank statements in PDF, Excel or CSV and turns them into organized transactions, with balance and overlapping-period checks.
  • Prepares the journal, ledger, trial balance and the client's fiscal years, with one Excel workbook per year to review.
  • An assistant answers questions and prepares work (allocating pending items, proposing entries): everything stays a draft, and it never closes a fiscal year on its own.
  • Covers reconciliation, inflation adjustment (RT6/RECPAM), Argentina's Law 25,413 and up-to-date FACPCE indexes.

To protect the work, this page shows what the project solves, not how it is built inside.

Project status

In pilot stage, being tested with a limited set of real cases.
